# Privacy Policy for Pinoria

**Effective Date:** October 7, 2026

## 1. Introduction
**Pinoria** is a local-first browser extension designed to provide native-feeling download and media organization capabilities for Pinterest. We believe privacy is a fundamental human right.

## 2. Zero Data Collection
Pinoria does NOT collect, track, store, or transmit any personally identifiable information, browsing history, account credentials, or telemetry data. 

- **No Remote Servers:** Pinoria operates 100% locally on your computer. It has no external analytics, tracker, or cloud servers.
- **No Third-Party Sharing:** Since no data is collected, no information is ever sold, rented, or transferred to any third party.

## 3. Permissions Justification
Pinoria requests only the minimal permissions required to function:
- **`downloads`**: Used exclusively to save requested images, GIFs, and videos to your local computer's Downloads directory.
- **`offscreen`**: Used to process and mux video/audio streams into MP4 files locally using browser Web APIs without leaving your computer.
- **`storage`**: Used exclusively to save your local UI preferences (such as custom destination folder names and category colors) on your device.
- **Host Permissions (`*.pinterest.com`, `*.pinimg.com`)**: Required to identify Pins and download media files directly from Pinterest's official media CDN.

## 4. User Control & Data Retention
All configuration data (custom folder paths, color settings) is stored strictly in your browser's local storage and can be modified or cleared at any time in the extension popup or by uninstalling the extension.
