KILLER FEATURE

Download Pinterest Videos with Full High-Fidelity Audio

Ever downloaded a Pinterest video from random online websites only to discover it had no sound?

Pinterest separates audio and video into isolated HLS streams. Pinoria automatically captures both streams and merges them locally inside your browser using hardware-accelerated Web APIs. You get a pristine, original 1080p MP4 file with crystal-clear audio every single time.

  • Automatic audio & video stream synchronization
  • 100% offline processing via native browser offscreen documents
  • Zero compression or quality loss
BATCH AUTOMATION

1-Click Batch Download for Entire Boards & Sections

Stop right-clicking pins one by one. Archive your favorite aesthetic moodboards effortlessly.

Pinoria embeds a native red "Download Board" button right next to the Pinterest board search bar. Click once, and Pinoria's smart virtual feed scanner scrolls, eliminates duplicates, and downloads every item sequentially into an organized folder.

  • Auto-pauses when you interact, scrolls in safe human batches
  • Visible live progress indicators (e.g., "Downloading 48/50 items")
  • Supports both public collections and your private secret boards

FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

Got Questions? We've Got Answers.

Can Pinoria download Pinterest videos with sound?

Yes, absolutely! Pinoria has a dedicated client-side audio/video muxing engine built with Web APIs. It captures the separate audio and video tracks from Pinterest CDN and stitches them into a standard high-definition MP4 with crystal-clear audio.

How do I download an entire Pinterest board at once?

Simply navigate to any board or section page on Pinterest. Pinoria places a clean red "Download Board" button right next to the board search input. Click it, and Pinoria will automatically scroll through the board, detect all pins, eliminate duplicates, and download them sequentially.

Does Pinoria download images in original quality?

Yes. Pinoria inspects the image URL and resolves it to the largest available original asset hosted on Pinterest's official media CDN (originals/ directory), guaranteeing that you get crisp HD/4K resolution rather than feed thumbnails.

Is Pinoria free and safe? Does it track my activity?

Pinoria is 100% free and open-source on GitHub. It operates completely local-first: zero external servers, zero analytics, and zero tracking. Your data and downloads never leave your browser.

Does Pinoria work with private (secret) boards?

Yes! Because Pinoria runs directly in your active browser session as an extension, it can seamlessly download any secret or private board that your logged-in account has permission to view.
